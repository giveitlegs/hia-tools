# Home Inspector Website Template

A free one-page website template for home inspectors. Enter your business name, city, services, prices and service area, pick a color theme, and preview the site on desktop and phone. Then download a ready-to-upload index.html with your title tag, meta description and local business structured data already filled in. No sign-up, no builder subscription.

## How to use the website template

1. Enter your business name, city, state, phone, email and license number.
2. Add the other cities you serve, a short tagline and a paragraph about you.
3. Choose your services and starting prices, or hide prices.
4. Pick a color theme and preview the site on desktop and phone sizes.
5. Download index.html and upload it to any web host, or copy the HTML into your site builder.

## What the template includes

- **A hero** with your city in the main heading and a booking button that links to your scheduler, or calls you.
- **Services and prices** for the general inspection and the add-ons you offer.
- **A service area section** listing the cities you cover.
- **About and FAQ sections** answering what clients ask before they book.
- **Contact details** with tap-to-call and email links.
- **Local SEO basics:** a title tag and meta description with your city, Open Graph tags, and ProfessionalService structured data with your service area and offers.

## What makes a home inspector website book jobs

- **Put the booking button at the top.** Most visitors are buyers on a deadline, many on a phone.
- **Show your license number and service area.** Buyers and agents check both before they call.
- **Publish starting prices.** It filters out price shoppers and builds trust with the rest.
- **Link a sample report.** Agents and buyers want to see how you write. Our [sample home inspection report](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/pages/sample-home-inspection-report/) shows what a good one looks like.
- **Make one page per city you serve** once the basics are live. That is where local search traffic comes from.
- **Keep your directory listings consistent** with the same name, phone and address. [Claim your Inspector Atlas listing](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/pages/claim-your-listing/) free to fix your details.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### Is this home inspector website template free?
Yes. You can preview, download and use the template for free with no sign-up. You only pay for your own domain and hosting.
:::

::: faq
### How do I put the template online?
Upload the downloaded index.html to any web host, such as your domain registrar's hosting, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. You can also paste the sections into a page in WordPress or another builder.
:::

::: faq
### Can I use it with WordPress?
Yes. Paste the HTML into a Custom HTML block, or use the sections as a guide for your theme. The title tag, meta description and structured data go in your SEO plugin's settings.
:::

::: faq
### Does the template include SEO?
It includes a city-specific title tag, meta description, a single H1, Open Graph tags and ProfessionalService structured data with your service area and services. You still need a Google Business Profile and consistent directory listings.
:::

::: faq
### Are my details saved?
They are saved in this browser only, so you can come back and edit. Nothing is sent to Inspector Atlas.
:::
