# Home Inspector Salary by State

Construction and building inspectors, the federal job category that includes home inspectors, earned a median of **$74,690** a year in May 2025, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The middle half earned $58,860 to $94,710. Pay is highest in Alaska, California and Connecticut. Use the calculator to estimate what a self-employed inspector could net in your state.

## Methodology and limits

- Wages come from BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS), May 2025, for SOC 47-4011. BLS SOC 47-4011 covers construction and building inspectors, including but not limited to home inspectors.
- OEWS counts employees only. Self-employed inspectors are not in these numbers, and many home inspectors are self-employed.
- The income calculator multiplies inspections per week by weeks worked and by your fee plus add-ons, then subtracts the expenses you enter. Expense defaults are placeholders, not survey data. It does not deduct income tax or self-employment tax.
- National outlook: BLS projects little or no change (0%) for 2025-35, with about 153,900 jobs.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### How much does a home inspector make?
BLS reports a median of $74,690 a year (May 2025) for construction and building inspectors, the category that includes home inspectors. The top 10% earned more than $114,200. Self-employed inspectors are not counted, and their income depends on volume, fees and expenses.
:::

::: faq
### Which state pays home inspectors the most?
Alaska has the highest median at $102,410, followed by California ($101,290) and Connecticut ($98,670).
:::

::: faq
### How much do self-employed home inspectors make?
It depends on how many inspections you do, what you charge and your costs. For example, 6 inspections a week for 48 weeks at $475 including add-ons is about $137,000 in revenue before expenses and taxes. Use the calculator with your own numbers.
:::

::: faq
### Is the BLS number only for home inspectors?
No. SOC 47-4011 covers construction and building inspectors, including municipal code inspectors and other building inspectors. It is the closest federal wage series for home inspectors.
:::

::: faq
### How many inspections does a home inspector do per week?
Full-time inspectors commonly book one or two inspections a day. Volume is seasonal and follows local home sales.
:::

::: faq
### Do home inspectors need a license to earn money?
In most states, yes. Check our license requirements table before you start marketing your services.
:::
