# Home Inspection Invoice Template

A free home inspection invoice template for inspectors. Add your business and client details, pick the inspection and any add-on services, apply a discount or sales tax, record payments, and get a clean invoice you can print, save as a PDF, or download as a Word or spreadsheet file. Your business details are remembered in this browser for the next invoice.

## How to use the invoice template

1. Enter your company name, address, phone, email and license number.
2. Set the invoice number, date and payment terms, then add the client and the inspected property.
3. Add services from the list or type your own. Change the preset rates to your fees.
4. Add a discount, sales tax if your state taxes inspection services, and any amount already paid.
5. Print or save a PDF, or download a Word (.doc) or spreadsheet (.csv) copy. **Start next invoice** keeps your business details and moves to the next number.

## What to put on a home inspection invoice

- Your business name, contact details and license number.
- A unique invoice number, the invoice date and the due date or terms.
- The client's name and billing address, and the inspected property's address and inspection date.
- One line per service: the inspection, each ancillary test, re-inspections, travel and size surcharges.
- Subtotal, discounts, tax, payments received and the balance due.
- How to pay, and whether the report is released on payment.

## Getting paid

- Collect payment at or before the inspection, or before the report is released. State this in your [pre-inspection agreement](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/pages/home-inspection-contract/).
- If a closing agent pays the fee at closing, put the property address and closing date on the invoice so escrow can match it.
- Most inspection software can take card payments and send invoices automatically. A template is useful when you are starting out or for one-off jobs.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### Is this invoice template free?
Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark. Your details are saved only in this browser.
:::

::: faq
### Can I download the invoice as a PDF?
Yes. Click Print / save PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. You can also download a Word (.doc) file or a spreadsheet (.csv).
:::

::: faq
### Do I charge sales tax on a home inspection?
Most states do not tax home inspection services, but a few tax some professional services. Check with your state's revenue department or your accountant.
:::

::: faq
### When should a home inspector send the invoice?
Send it with the booking confirmation or at the inspection, and collect payment before or at the time the report is delivered. Put your payment terms in your pre-inspection agreement too.
:::

::: faq
### How do I number my invoices?
Use a simple sequence, such as 1001, 1002 and so on. The template remembers your last number in this browser and moves to the next one when you start a new invoice.
:::
