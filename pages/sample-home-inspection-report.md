## How to read a home inspection report

- **Start with the summary**, then read the full section behind each item. Summaries leave out context.
- **Check the limitations.** Every report lists areas the inspector could not see, such as a blocked crawlspace or a roof viewed only from the ground.
- **Read the recommendation line.** "Evaluate by a licensed electrician" means the inspector found a symptom, not the full cause or cost. Get that evaluation before your [contingency deadline](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/inspection-contingency-calculator/).
- **Look at the photos.** A good report shows each defect, which helps your agent, the seller and contractors.
- **Separate maintenance from defects.** Old but working equipment is not a defect on its own, but it is a budgeting item.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### What does a home inspection report include?
A description of the property and conditions on the day, the scope and limitations, a summary of material defects, and section-by-section findings for the roof, exterior, structure, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, attic, interior and garage, usually with photos and recommendations.
:::

::: faq
### How long is a typical home inspection report?
Many run 30 to 60 pages with photos. The summary is usually a few pages.
:::

::: faq
### How soon do you get the report?
Most inspectors deliver it the same day or within 24 to 48 hours. Some states set a delivery deadline.
:::

::: faq
### What do the severity ratings mean?
Most reports sort findings into safety hazards, items needing repair or replacement, routine maintenance, and items to monitor. The exact labels vary by inspector and software.
:::

::: faq
### Is there a free home inspection report template?
Yes. Download the blank template from the tool above. It opens in Word or Google Docs and follows the same section layout.
:::

::: faq
### Can I share the report with the seller?
Yes. Buyers commonly share the relevant pages with the seller with a repair request. The report belongs to the client who paid for it.
:::
