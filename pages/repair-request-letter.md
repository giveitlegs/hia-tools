# Home Inspection Repair Request Letter Builder

A repair request letter lists the defects from your home inspection report and asks the seller to fix each one, give you a credit at closing, or lower the price. Build yours below: pick items from a list or paste them from your report, choose the ask for each, set a response date, then copy, print or download the letter. Everything stays in your browser.

## How to use the letter builder

1. Enter the buyer and seller names, the property address, and the contract and inspection dates.
2. Add items from the common-defects list, or paste lines from your report (one per line).
3. For each item, choose **Repair**, **Closing credit** or **Price reduction**, and enter an amount for credits.
4. Set a response date before your [inspection contingency deadline](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/inspection-contingency-calculator/).
5. Copy the letter, save it as a PDF, or download a .doc. Send it to your agent, who puts the final terms on your state's form.

## What to ask for

- **Repairs** work for safety items and anything a lender requires. Ask for licensed contractors and receipts.
- **A closing credit** lets you hire your own contractor after closing. Lenders cap credits, so check with your loan officer.
- **A price reduction** lowers the loan amount and the purchase price. It helps most with big-ticket items like a roof or HVAC system.
- Focus on safety hazards and material defects, not cosmetic or maintenance items. Sellers respond better to a short, specific list with contractor quotes attached.

## Common items buyers include

- **Safety:** Missing GFCI protection at kitchen, bathroom, garage or exterior outlets; Double-tapped breakers and open knockouts in the main electrical panel; Missing or inoperable smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
- **Roof and exterior:** Damaged, lifted or missing roof shingles; Failed or missing flashing at the chimney or roof-to-wall joints; Deck ledger board not bolted or flashed.
- **Structure and moisture:** Active water intrusion in the basement or crawlspace; Soft or water-damaged subfloor; Evidence of wood-destroying insect activity or damage.
- **Systems:** Active plumbing leak under a sink or at a toilet; Heating system did not respond to the thermostat; Air conditioning system not cooling adequately; evaluation by an HVAC contractor recommended.

## State repair and amendment forms

| State | Form |
|---|---|
| Arizona | AAR Buyer's Inspection Notice and Seller's Response (BINSR) |
| California | C.A.R. Form RR (Request for Repair) and RRRR (Request for Repair, Seller Response and Buyer Reply); contingency removal on C.A.R. Form CR |
| Colorado | Colorado Real Estate Commission NTC43 Inspection Objection Notice (mandatory use January 1, 2026), resolved by a signed Inspection Resolution |
| Florida | No separate statewide repair form. AS IS contract para 12 (inspection and right to cancel only, no seller repair duty); FR/Bar standard contract Inspection and Repair provisions with Repair Limits; changes by written amendment to the contract |
| Georgia | GAR F704 Amendment to Address Concerns with Property (F710 Amendment to Change the Due Diligence Period) |
| Illinois | Multi-Board 8.0 para 14(b) written Notice of major-component defects; Chicago Association of REALTORS Inspection Contingency Rider (2025) |
| North Carolina | NC REALTORS Form 310-T Due Diligence Request and Agreement (repairs only; credits go on Form 4-T Agreement to Amend Contract) |
| Ohio | Columbus REALTORS RES02 Buyer Request to Remedy (11/24); Ohio REALTORS contract para 9.C request to remedy |
| Pennsylvania | PAR Written Corrective Proposal / reply to inspection under ASR para 13 Inspection Contingency (form name not confirmed) |
| Tennessee | Tennessee REALTORS RF655 Repair/Replacement Amendment; RF656 Notification; RF653 Amendment |
| Texas | TREC Amendment to Contract, TREC No. 39-11 (effective 07/01/2026; replaces 39-10) |
| Utah | REPC Due Diligence Objection and resolution by addendum (UAR Addendum to REPC) |
| Virginia | Virginia REALTORS Form 600D Home Inspection Contingency Addendum (removal/negotiation addendum); NVAR K1343 Inspection Contingency Removal Addendum (01/26) |
| Washington | NWMLS Form 35R Inspection Response (with Form 35 Inspection Addendum) |

In most transactions, the binding agreement is the signed addendum or amendment, not a letter. Use the letter to organize your request and share it with your agent.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### How do I write a repair request after a home inspection?
List each defect with its location and the page in the inspection report, say whether you want a repair, a credit or a price reduction, attach contractor quotes for large items, and set a response date before your contingency deadline.
:::

::: faq
### Should I ask for repairs or a credit?
Credits give you control over who does the work and how. Repairs make sense for safety items and anything your lender requires before closing.
:::

::: faq
### What should not go on a repair request?
Cosmetic items, routine maintenance, and things you knew about when you made your offer. A long list of small items can sour the negotiation.
:::

::: faq
### Does the seller have to make repairs?
Not usually. In most states the seller can agree, refuse or counter. Your contract's inspection contingency sets what happens if you cannot agree.
:::

::: faq
### Do I need a special form?
Many states and local associations have their own repair request or amendment form, such as TREC's Amendment to Contract in Texas or C.A.R.'s Request for Repair in California. Your agent will use the right one.
:::

::: faq
### Is my letter saved?
It is saved in this browser only so you can come back to it. Nothing is sent to Inspector Atlas.
:::
