# Home Inspection Contract Template

A home inspection contract, also called a pre-inspection agreement, sets out what you will inspect, what you will not, your fee, and how disputes are handled. Build yours below: add your business and client details, choose your standards of practice and clauses, check your state's notes, then print it or download a Word copy. **This is not legal advice. Have an attorney licensed in your state review your agreement before you use it.**

## How to use the contract builder

1. Enter your company, inspector name, license number and state. Your state's licensing notes appear below the form.
2. Add the client, property address, inspection date and fee, and choose when payment is due.
3. Pick the standards of practice the inspection follows: your state's, InterNACHI's or ASHI's.
4. Add any ancillary services, such as radon, WDO or sewer scope, with their fees.
5. Turn optional clauses on or off and set the limits, then print, save a PDF or download a .doc for your attorney.

## What a pre-inspection agreement usually covers

- **Scope:** a visual, non-invasive inspection of readily accessible systems, under a named standard of practice.
- **Fee and payment terms,** including ancillary services.
- **What the inspection is not:** not a warranty, code inspection, appraisal or environmental test.
- **Exclusions:** mold, radon, pests, pools, septic and other items unless added as a service.
- **Access and attendance:** utilities on, areas accessible, client invited to the walk-through.
- **Notice of claims and right to re-inspect** before a disputed condition is repaired.
- **Limitation of liability and a time limit for claims,** where state law allows them.
- **Dispute resolution:** mediation, arbitration or court.
- **Third-party use** of the report, cancellation terms and signatures.

## State rules to check first

Contract rules for inspectors vary a lot. Some states require a signed written agreement before the inspection. Some restrict or void clauses that limit the inspector's liability, and some regulate arbitration clauses or the time to bring a claim. California's Business and Professions Code, for example, voids contract terms that limit a home inspector's liability. Pick your state in the builder to see the notes from our [home inspector license requirements](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/pages/home-inspector-license-requirements/) data, then confirm the details with a local attorney or your E&O carrier. Your [insurance](https://homeinspectoratlas.com/pages/home-inspector-insurance/) provider may also have required contract language.

## Tips for using your agreement

- Send it with the booking confirmation and get it signed before you arrive. Most report software can collect e-signatures.
- Keep the signed agreement with the report for as long as your state's record-retention rule requires.
- Use the same standard of practice in the agreement and the report.
- Review the template with an attorney each year, and whenever your state changes its rules.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### Is this home inspection contract template legally binding?
It is a starting template, not legal advice. Whether an agreement is enforceable depends on your state's law and how it is signed. Have an attorney licensed in your state review it before you use it.
:::

::: faq
### Do home inspectors need a pre-inspection agreement?
Many states require a written agreement signed before the inspection, and most E&O insurers expect one. Even where it is not required, it sets expectations and protects both sides.
:::

::: faq
### Can a home inspector limit liability to the inspection fee?
In some states, yes. Others restrict or void limitation of liability clauses, including California. Courts in several states have also refused to enforce them in some cases. Ask a local attorney.
:::

::: faq
### When should the client sign the agreement?
Before the inspection starts. Send it with the booking confirmation so the client has time to read it, and do not begin the inspection until it is signed.
:::

::: faq
### What is the difference between a home inspection contract and a purchase contract inspection contingency?
The inspection contract is between the client and the inspector. The inspection contingency is part of the home purchase contract between buyer and seller and sets the deadline to inspect and negotiate repairs.
:::

::: faq
### Is my agreement saved?
It is saved in this browser only, so you can come back to it. Nothing is sent to Inspector Atlas.
:::
