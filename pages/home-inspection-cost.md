# Home Inspection Cost Calculator

A standard home inspection for a single-family house typically costs **$300 to $500**, with most published averages between $340 and $400. Size, age, location and add-on tests like radon or a sewer scope change the price. Enter your home's details below for an estimate, then compare quotes from local inspectors.

## How to use the calculator

1. Choose your state. The estimate is adjusted by the state's price level (BEA Regional Price Parities). You can switch the adjustment off.
2. Pick the property type and enter the square footage and year built.
3. Tick any add-on tests you need. Each shows its published price range.
4. Open "How this was calculated" to see every assumption used.

## Average cost by home size

| Home size | Typical price |
|---|---|
| 0–999 sq ft | $200 – $250 |
| 1,000–1,500 sq ft | $250 – $300 |
| 1,501–2,000 sq ft | $300 – $400 |
| 2,001–2,500 sq ft | $400 – $450 |
| 2,501–3,000 sq ft | $450 – $500 |

Above 3,000 sq ft, many inspectors add roughly $25 for every extra 500 sq ft (Angi). Older homes (built before 1980) can add $100 to $200 because there is more to check (HomeGuide). Crawlspaces and basements also add time, though sources publish no fixed dollar figure.

## Methodology

The calculator starts from published size bands (HomeGuide) for houses and townhomes, or from published ranges for condos, manufactured homes, multi-family buildings and new construction. It adds the published older-home surcharge for homes built before 1980 and the ranges for each add-on you tick, then multiplies everything by the state price level if you leave that option on. Figures are rounded to the nearest $5. We did not invent any multiplier. All inputs and sources are in our public [dataset](https://github.com/giveitlegs/hia-tools/blob/main/data/cost.json), checked 2026-11-21.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
### How much does a home inspection cost?
Most published sources put a standard single-family inspection at about $300 to $500, with averages of $340 to $400. Larger, older and rural homes cost more, and add-on tests are extra.
:::

::: faq
### Who pays for the home inspection?
In most US home sales the buyer orders and pays for the inspection, usually at the appointment or before the report is released. Sellers sometimes pay for a pre-listing inspection.
:::

::: faq
### Is a home inspection included in closing costs?
Usually not. You pay the inspector directly before closing, even if the sale falls through. Some lenders let you list it on the closing disclosure as paid outside closing.
:::

::: faq
### Why do some inspectors charge more?
Price follows the home's size, age and foundation type, travel distance, the inspector's experience and tools, and any add-on tests. A low price can mean a shorter inspection or a thinner report, so compare sample reports.
:::

::: faq
### How much does a radon test add?
Published ranges run about $125 to $400 for a radon test, depending on the method and whether it is booked with the home inspection.
:::

::: faq
### Does a condo inspection cost less?
Usually. Published condo ranges start around $200 because common areas like the roof and structure are often outside the unit inspection.
:::

::: faq
### Can I get a home inspection for free?
Not a real one. Free offers are usually tied to a sale or a repair contractor. An independent inspector you pay directly works for you.
:::
